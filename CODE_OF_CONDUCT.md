# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free environment for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith.
- Give technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect differing experience levels.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing and sexually explicit or hostile conduct are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report serious conduct concerns privately to the maintainers.
